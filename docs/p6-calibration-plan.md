# P6 known-scene calibration plan

The P6 projection and elevation-risk implementations pass deterministic
fixtures, and the accepted Phase L run exercises the live RGB-D-to-map path.
The final seeded Gazebo simulator batch passes gate
`p6-sim-calibration-v2-20261117`. It uses three independent captures on a
40 m known plane for projection and flat-layer checks, plus one separate
seeded-crater capture for bowl geometry. The recorder uses Gazebo's settled
full 3D rover pose and matches delayed segmentation masks to the closest depth
frame. Physical camera/terrain qualification remains separate from this
simulator-only gate.

## P6-01: RGB-D projection

1. Use the procedural Gazebo world as the reference scene. Record generator
   seed, world/model SDF hashes, terrain mesh hashes, the rover pose, and the
   map-frame coordinates/heights of visible targets. Prefer fixed SDF targets
   or analytically known terrain points across near, middle, and far image
   regions; retain the generator's exact source coordinates and interpolation
   method as truth provenance.
2. Record camera intrinsics, RGB/depth registration, camera-to-base transform,
   sensor pitch, timestamps, and the exact software/model hashes used.
3. Use the frozen point count, depth bins, and p95/maximum projection limits
   from `config/p6_calibration_gates.json`. Bound reference-coordinate error
   by the authored SDF/mesh resolution and report that bound alongside results.
4. Capture fresh ROS/Gazebo RGB-D frames, project target points through the
   production mapper, and compare output map coordinates against seeded
   geometry. Retain per-point residuals, exclusions, timestamps, actual
   Gazebo rover pose, and configuration hashes. Repeat after a clean process
   restart.

## P6-03: slope and bowl risk

1. Use exact analytic terrain from the seeded generator for a flat reference
   and bowl geometry; add a known planar ramp in a calibration-only SDF if the
   generated terrain does not provide a sufficiently uniform incline. Record
   ground-truth elevation grids, slope, curvature, and mesh discretization
   independently of the mapper.
2. Use the frozen slope error, roughness, bowl-risk, flat false-positive, and
   minimum coverage limits from `config/p6_calibration_gates.json`. Do not tune
   them against the qualification trial.
3. Capture RGB-D traverses at the configured sensor resolution and speed.
   Compare the emitted elevation-derived slope, roughness, crater-risk, and
   uncertainty layers to the surveyed references cell by cell after applying
   the documented map registration.
4. Retain raw sensor samples, reference data, aligned output grids, per-layer
   errors, map/status logs, model/config hashes, and a clean-restart repeat.
   If a gate fails, tune only on a separate calibration scene and repeat with
   the frozen qualification scene.

## Latest calibration result

Final evidence: `evidence/runs/20261117-p6-final-v3/summary.json` (**PASS**;
4 captures). All three plane captures passed both depth bands and flat-layer
checks. Across those runs, horizontal p95 was 0.0219 m in 1.5-3 m and 0.0576 m
in 3-5 m; vertical p95 was 0.0096 m and 0.0152 m. Their worst flat slope-risk
p95 was 2 points and crater-risk p95 was 0.

The seeded crater capture passed with 157 valid cells in the configured
0.7 m bowl-center region and crater-risk max 100 (minimum 70). Slope, roughness,
and crater-risk p95 errors were 9, 14.2, and 0 points. Projection checks are
skipped for this geometry-only capture; the three independent plane captures
are the projection qualification runs. Accuracy and coverage thresholds were
not relaxed.

The calibration fixes were: a generated isolated flat scene, full 3D settled
pose registration (including roll and pitch), nearest-stamp depth matching,
local-plane slope fitting over 1 m to reduce depth-noise amplification, and a
sparse 3 m rim sample that detects the broad seeded crater floor. The
calibration-only plane scene is generated by
`tools/generate_p6_known_plane.py`; operational terrain remains in the seeded
crater capture. Full hardware qualification remains separate.
